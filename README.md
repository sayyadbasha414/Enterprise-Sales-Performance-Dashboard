📊 Global Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project provides a complete business performance analysis covering Sales, Customers, Products, and Delivery Operations. The dashboard enables management teams to monitor revenue growth, customer behavior, profitability, order performance, and operational efficiency through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

🎯 Business Objectives
The dashboard aims to:
Monitor overall sales performance.
Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
Track product profitability.
Evaluate regional sales performance.
Monitor delivery efficiency.
Identify top-performing customers and products.
Support strategic business decisions using data-driven insights.
